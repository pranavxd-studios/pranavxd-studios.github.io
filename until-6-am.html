About the game

Welcome to UNTIL 6 AM, a thrilling horror survival experience where every hour brings new challenges, unexpected events and terrifying surprises.

Your shift isn’t over yet. Explore your surroundings, stay alert, pay attention to strange occurrences and uncover the mystery behind the night. Every decision matters. Trust your instincts and make it through the night.

What you can do

  • Every hour brings new events
  • Hunt anomalies across 6 CCTV cameras
  • Watch out for the Visitor
  • Manage power and stability
  • Use the emergency light and security shutter
  • Collect clues in the investigation notebook
