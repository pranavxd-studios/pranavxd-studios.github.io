About the game

You wake up inside a mysterious, cursed house with no idea what happened. It’s dark, abandoned and full of disturbing secrets. Something is watching you, and escaping won’t be easy.

Explore, search for clues, solve puzzles and unlock doors to uncover the truth. Use your flashlight wisely, find places to hide and don’t get caught.

What you can do

  • Explore a mysterious, cursed house
  • Light the dark with your flashlight
  • Solve puzzles and unlock hidden areas
  • Hide from a terrifying entity
  • Discover secrets and find the way out
  • Reach multiple endings
